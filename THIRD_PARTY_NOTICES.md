## Optional 3D Dice Renderer

VeilFarers Player Tool can optionally load the `sarahRosannaBusch/dice` renderer at runtime from jsDelivr.

- Project: `sarahRosannaBusch/dice`
- Repository: https://github.com/sarahRosannaBusch/dice
- License: MIT
- Copyright: © 2022 dice authors
- Authors and credits identified by the project: Sarah Rosanna Busch; derived from work by Anton Natarov (Teal); uses three.js and cannon.js.

The renderer is not required for the standard 2D roller. Selecting 3D mode requests the renderer files from the external CDN. If they cannot be loaded, Player Tool falls back to its standard dice display and still resolves the roll locally.
