VEILFARERS PORTAL v59.71 — START HERE
=====================================

AUTHORITATIVE BASELINE
----------------------
Continue from v59.71. It supersedes v59.70 for all Player Tool and Creator UI work.

WHAT THIS RELEASE DOES
----------------------
- Restores the Player Tool proportions and visual language of v59.56.
- Keeps the v59.70 authentication and interrupted-dice stabilization.
- Makes every class feature in Player Tool schematic, mechanical and concise.
- Adds selected-card lift and a gold [+] full-information reverse to Creator cards.
- Makes recommendations more visible, including Equipment.
- Keeps Human Inheritance of the Builders entirely in Equipment.
- Keeps White Stone disabled; VeilFarers Blue is the only active theme.

DEPLOY
------
1. Extract VeilFarers_Portal_v59.71.zip.
2. Deploy the extracted package root to a Netlify Deploy Preview.
3. Do not deploy a parent folder containing the package root.
4. Confirm /data/deploy-version.json reports v59.71.
5. Confirm the HTML response header reports X-VeilFarers-Release: v59.71.
6. Hard refresh or use a private window to avoid cached v59.70 CSS/JS.

PLAYER TOOL ACCEPTANCE
----------------------
- Compare the HUD directly with the v59.56 reference.
- Numeric fields show discreet custom chevrons, never native browser spinners.
- Experience rows remain separate and do not overlap at 100%, 125% or 200% zoom.
- Resource tracks are compact and preserve filled/empty/unavailable states.
- Test Awakened and Sleeping variants of all nine classes.
- Class cards contain only actionable mechanics; no flavor or editorial commentary.
- Verify the title and summary of each card match the selected Class and Path.

CREATOR ACCEPTANCE
------------------
- Selecting Ancestry, Community, Class or Affiliation visibly raises that card.
- Cards with shortened/clipped copy show a gold [+] at bottom-right.
- [+] moves the card forward and flips to complete text without artwork.
- [−] returns to the front.
- Opening a second reverse closes the first.
- Recommendations are obvious in Traits, Powers and Equipment.

HUMAN WORKFLOW
--------------
Select Human in Ancestry. In Equipment:
1. choose The Graft or The Tool;
2. choose the exact inherited augmentation/item;
3. choose normal starting weapon and armor.
The inherited item is free and does not consume normal starting budgets.

LIVE TABLE / DICE ACCEPTANCE
----------------------------
- Run a 3D roll, click during motion, close deliberately, and run another roll.
- Click the overlay after settlement and confirm it closes.
- Sign in through Netlify Identity.
- Connect Keeper Tool and Player Tool to the same table.
- Confirm Keeper changes and notes reach the Player Tool.
- Expire the session and confirm polling stops instead of repeating 401 requests.
- Sign in again and reconnect successfully.
