Your life beyond the Veil.

A complete science-fiction expansion for crews who want strange worlds, hard choices and the freedom to build a life among the stars.

Seven Domains · New powers

Choose how you touch the impossible.

Each class opens a Domain Pair. Powers can be prepared into an active Loadout, creating science-fiction abilities with a distinct identity rather than one universal spell list.

Tech

Machines, systems, drones, intrusion and the precise language of engineered possibility.

Synthesis

Life, adaptation, healing and the designed resilience of bodies and ecosystems.

Social

Influence, connection, perception and the power carried through trust, identity and story.

Neural

Thought, perception, memory and the hidden architecture linking minds across the dark.

Kinetic

Force, momentum, impact and the decisive control of energy in motion.

Void

Distance, concealment, gravity and the strange intervals where ordinary space loses authority.

Lux

Radiance, guidance, revelation and the Hope Frequency made visible in the world.

The Six Houses

Power has a name and a crest.

House affiliation shapes a character's professional life, obligations and reputation. Each institution offers a place to belong—and a cost for belonging there.

Keepers of Wisdom

House Amaranth

Archivists and scholars who preserve civilization across long cycles of collapse and recovery. Knowledge is their inheritance, and withholding it can become a form of theft.

The Sentinels

House Joric

Defenders shaped by duty, discipline and the promise to stand between danger and the vulnerable. Their hardest stories begin when protocol and protection diverge.

The God-Makers

House Kurogane

Engineers, physicians and researchers who believe progress can be built. Their brilliance changes bodies and worlds, often behind doors even their own specialists cannot open.

The Fading Crown

House Solari

Heirs to ceremony, beauty and the obligations of an older order. Their courts remain luminous even as the authority beneath those traditions quietly recedes.

The Pearl Merchants

House Valerius

Traders and contract-makers who measure reliability, risk and return across the galaxy. Their most important debts are the ones no ledger can price.

The War-Makers

House Volkov

A martial power built around survival, strength and the refusal to be conquered. Loyalty matters because abandoning an ally is weakness made public.
