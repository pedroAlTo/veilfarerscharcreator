A / First contact

The threshold.

Freight docks, cargo rails and berths built in different eras. The automatic systems still respond. That is not the same as a welcome.

B / Public concourse

The ghost of a working day.

Market stalls, repair workshops and a cantina called Last Call. Ordinary places become clues to the people who used to fill them.

C / Living quarters

Someone called this home.

Crew quarters, family pods, a shared galley and a medical bay. Beyond the machinery, the refuge holds the traces of a community.

D / Station systems

Power has a price.

Reactors, auxiliary systems and service catwalks. Keeping a station alive takes more than finding the right switch.

E / Research access

Every archive keeps something.

Server nodes, observation spaces and security partitions. The research spine connects the refuge to the work that brought it here.
