Behind the screen · Keeper's Companion

Make the galaxy answer back.

A practical guide to running a VeilFarers space opera campaign with cinematic pace, human stakes and worlds that feel alive.

The Keeper's Companion turns the setting into playable scenes: create memorable NPCs, build pressure with clocks, layer environments, design encounters and reveal the secrets that move the Pearl Era from behind the curtain.

Clocks

Race, faction, investigation, complex-task and chase clocks turn invisible pressure into a readable narrative engine.

Demo: a reactor cooling, isolation or repair roll with Failure with Fear—or a printed reactor feature—marks a segment. At six, resolve the declared meltdown. Time alone does not advance this clock.
