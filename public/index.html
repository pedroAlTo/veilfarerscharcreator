A briefing for your first crew

Daggerheart in space.

VeilFarers is a free space opera TTRPG expansion compatible with Daggerheart. Build a crew, cross the Veil and find a place in a galaxy of ancient legacies, rival Houses and unfamiliar cultures.

Its science-fiction rules connect the personal stakes of a tabletop role-playing game to the scale of interstellar adventure: the ship you call home, the people who trust you and the technology that can change a world.

Start with the free First Crossing adventure ↗

What does it add to Daggerheart?

The VeilFarers Corebook introduces nine ancestries, nine classes, new powers, augmentations, a Wealth Tier economy and reputation with the galaxy’s factions. Starship combat gives every crew member a part in the battle.

What kind of space opera can we play?

Explore alien worlds, negotiate between rival powers or risk everything for your crew. Alien Species brings cultures and encounters; Rust & Steel brings advanced equipment and its consequences. The Keeper’s Companion helps turn them into a campaign.

Where should a new group begin?

First Crossing teaches the system through a 3–4 hour session for 3–5 players and one Keeper. Download the adventure and its required Table Kit, with five ready-to-play characters and reference sheets. Color maps are optional.

Is VeilFarers free and official?

The current books, starter adventure and play aids are free PDFs. VeilFarers is an independent community production, not an official Daggerheart release. See the compatibility and license credits.

How were the new mechanics designed?

Behind the Veil explores the decisions behind the rules. Start with Overkill and sci-fi damage thresholds, then try how Standard, Deadly and armor change the result of a hit.
