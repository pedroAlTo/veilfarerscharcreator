What is VeilFarers?

A shared story built for distant stars

Players create travellers shaped by ancestry, community, class and the choices they make together. One player becomes the Keeper, presenting a living universe, its dangers, and the people caught inside it.

The rules turn uncertainty into momentum. Hope opens opportunities. Fear gives the Keeper leverage. The story advances through victories, compromises and the costs the crew is willing to carry.
