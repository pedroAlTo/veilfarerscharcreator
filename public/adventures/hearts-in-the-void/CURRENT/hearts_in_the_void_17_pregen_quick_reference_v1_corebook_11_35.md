## The Duality Roll

Roll one **Hope d12** and one **Fear d12**, add the relevant Trait, and compare the total to the DC.

| DC | Difficulty |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | Routine under pressure |
| 10 | Easy |
| 15 | Challenging; standard consequential task |
| 20 | Difficult |
| 25 | Extreme |
| 30 | Nearly Impossible |

- Total meets or exceeds DC: success.
- Hope higher on a meaningful roll: mark 1 Effort Track segment, even on failure.
- Fear higher: the Keeper gains 1 Fear; success or failure still follows the total.
- Doubles: always a Critical Success. Complete/clear the Effort Track as applicable, clear 1 Stress, and place 1 point in Effort or Resolve. On an attack that hits, roll double damage dice; flat modifiers stay single.
- No uncertainty or meaningful consequence: do not roll, and do not mark the Effort Track.

## Effort and Resolve

**Effort** begins at 2, maximum 6. When both Effort Track segments are marked, clear them and gain 1 Effort. The track is the only normal Effort generator; Recovery does not create Effort.

At 0 Effort, a Sleeping character may pay 1 HP to invoke an Effort feature. They become **Burned Out**: maximum Stress is reduced by 1 until their next Recovery.

**Resolve** begins full at 2d4. Recover 1 die on a Short Rest and all dice on a Long Rest. Resolve adds only to totals; it never changes dominance or creates a critical.

- **Bear Down — spend and roll any number of Resolve Dice.** After a Duality, attack, reaction, or damage roll but before narration, add the total.

## Rest and Recovery

- **Short Rest:** with the personal Recovery Anchor available, clear 2 Stress; recover 1 Resolve Die. Other normal Short Rest choices, including Armor repair, apply separately.
- **Long Rest:** with the Anchor, clear all Stress; restore all Resolve. Recovery never generates Effort.
- If the Anchor is lost, broken, confiscated, refused, or left behind, no Recovery occurs until it is recovered or a new Anchor is established over a Long Rest.

## Attacks, Damage, and Armor

1. Roll Duality + weapon Trait against Evasion.
2. On a hit, roll the weapon expression. At Proficiency 1, roll the listed dice normally; never add the attack Trait to damage.
3. Below Major marks 1 HP; Major to below Severe marks 2 HP; Severe or above marks 3 HP. Every full 10 above Severe adds 1 HP.
4. Mark one Armor Slot to lower a hit by one severity: Overkill → Severe → Major → Minor → no HP.

Relevant weapon features in this crew:

- **Reliable:** +1 on the attack roll.
- **Armor Piercing:** reduce the target’s maximum Armor Slots by 1 until its next Long Rest; repeated applications stack.
- **Stunning:** a damaged target makes Strength against the Feature DC or is Stunned until the end of its next turn.
- **Poison:** when the attack causes HP loss, the target marks 1 Stress.
- **Concealable:** while concealed it adds no Burden; a routine search finds it only with Instinct DC 15.
- **Paired +1:** while wielding two Paired weapons, add +1 from one weapon to the damage of an attack with either; apply only one Paired bonus.
- **Flexible:** +1 Evasion, already included above.
- **Heavy:** −1 Evasion, already included for Marcus.
- **Jury-rigged:** when Marcus marks a Slot, roll d6; on 1 the armor malfunctions until repaired.

## Awakening During the Adventure

Awakening is an individual choice, never a group vote or mandatory upgrade.

1. Convert current Effort to current Hope one-for-one; maximum 6.
2. Switch off Effort Track, Burnout, Common Efforts, Honed Edge, Resolve/Moves, Recovery Anchor, and the Sleeping Effort Feature.
3. Activate the corresponding Awakened Hope Feature and the two Powers in that character’s Awakening Envelope.
4. Use the Awakened Class entry’s Spellcast Trait. A Mystic changes from Knowledge to Instinct and chooses Neural + Lux or Neural + Void; Lux and Void cannot both be prepared.
5. Experiences remain but now cost 1 Hope to invoke.
6. Do not heal HP, clear Stress, repair Armor, refresh resources, or erase Conditions.

The character remains the same person with the same Class, Subclass, Traits, Class Feature/Foundation, Connections, equipment, debts, injuries, and consequences. The player may replace either prepared Level 1 Power with another accessible Level 1 Power at the moment of Awakening.

## Keeper Teaching Notes

- **“I Need an Idea!”** is a terminal backup, not a reward for a bad roll. Introduce it only when resolved consequences—including completed clocks, if the scene actually uses them—have eliminated every conventional route. A clock advances only from its stated fictional triggers, never from unrelated bad luck. The protocol does not rewind clocks or erase costs.
- **Scene 8 Standoff:** put the dice away for the final social decision. A player can state or summarize the argument; do not ask for Persuasion. Return to normal rolls only if someone commits to violence or another uncertain action outside that decision.
