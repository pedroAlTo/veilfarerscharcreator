# VeilFarers v59.80 — legal and operational launch checklist

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice or a certification.

- Confirm the public controller details: Pedro Almagro Torrecillas, individual established in Poland, `pedro.alm.to@gmail.com`.
- Keep the minimum age at 16 and show the Terms and Privacy links before account creation.
- Do not publish the DNI/NIF.
- Keep Netlify Identity registration set to **Invite only**.
- Use Identity accounts with `player` or `keeper` roles for the private group; do not make ordinary users Netlify team collaborators.
- Restrict `admin` to the owner account and require the matching Functions-only `VF_ADMIN_EMAIL`.
- Confirm current Netlify and Google Cloud/Firebase processor terms and data-processing agreements.

- Test self-service and administrator account deletion, including Character Vault removal, membership removal, table ownership transfer and Identity-session invalidation.

- Delete resolved direct feedback within 12 months unless a documented legal or security reason requires longer retention.
- Keep a one-month workflow for data access, correction, export and deletion requests.
- Maintain an incident-response and personal-data-breach assessment process.
- Keep optional analytics, advertising and tracking disabled until a valid prior-consent mechanism exists.
- Review the legal notice, tax treatment and consumer information before activating Patreon, donations, paid access or subscriptions.
- Keep the official Daggerheart-compatible community logo unmodified and retain the exact DPCGL attribution.
- Base shared Daggerheart material on SRD 1.0 Public Game Content, not protected rulebook art or text.
- Recheck the DPCGL and whitelisted-VTT restriction before describing or operating the tools as a VTT or monetising access.
- Treat public interactive publication as a launch blocker until qualified counsel or Darrington Press confirms that the portal/Creator format is permitted.
- Run keyboard, contrast, zoom, reduced-motion and screen-reader checks; do not claim certified WCAG conformance without an audit.
