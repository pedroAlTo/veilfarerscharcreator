# Pregenerated Crew

## Canonical Level 1 Sleeping Characters for *Hearts in the Void*

This file replaces the earlier 11.2-based pregen blocks. It contains all ten pregenerated characters rebuilt from the supplied **VeilFarers Corebook 11.35 PDF Candidate full source**.

The historical sheets remain a source for names, voice, wounds, and relationships only. Their old Specialties, Conditions, statistics, Powers, weapons, and economic values are not rules sources.

Use this file with `Hearts_in_the_Void_17_Pregen_Quick_Reference_v1_Corebook_11_35.md`.

## Shared Starting Rules

Every pregen is:

- Level 1 and Sleeping;
- built from the fixed Trait array `+2, +1, +1, +0, +0, −1`;
- at 2 Effort of 6, with an empty two-segment Effort Track;
- at 2d4 Resolve, beginning the session full;
- Proficiency 1;
- equipped with class items, one Tier 1 weapon, one trained armor piece, and personal objects;
- Wealth Tier 0 with 0 uncommitted Credits;
- and given three Experiences at +2, invoked free once per roll while Sleeping.

The detailed Chapter 7 rules take precedence over older summary lines still present elsewhere in the 11.35 source: the two-segment track is the default Effort generator, Recovery never generates Effort, Experiences are free for Sleeping characters, and maximum Resolve is 2 at Level 1 and rises by 1 at Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9. A named Ancestry Gift that explicitly grants Effort, such as Stellar Meditation or Irrepressible Curiosity, is treated as a specific exception rather than as Recovery.

## The Awakening Envelope

Each character has two prepared Level 1 Domain Powers. They are **not active** until that individual character chooses to Wake.

When a pregen Awakens during the adventure:

1. current Effort becomes current Hope one-for-one, maximum 6;
2. the Effort Track, Burnout, Common Efforts, Honed Edge, Resolve, Sleeping Moves, and Recovery Anchor switch off;
3. the Sleeping Effort Feature becomes the class’s Awakened Hope Feature;
4. the two prepared Powers become the character’s starting Loadout;
5. use the Awakened Class entry’s Spellcast Trait; for Mystics this changes from Knowledge to Instinct and requires a Neural + Lux or Neural + Void alignment;
6. Experiences remain, but now cost 1 Hope to invoke;
7. Ancestry, Community, Affiliation, Class, Subclass, Traits, Class Feature, Subclass Foundation, Connections, gear, HP, Stress, Armor damage, and established consequences remain;
8. Awakening does not heal HP, clear Stress, repair Armor, refill resources, or erase a Condition.

The player may replace either prepared Power with another Level 1 Power from the character’s accessible Domains at the moment of Awakening.

> **11.35 text note.** Adaptive Spirit and Modular Consciousness still print “Hope” inside ancestry rules that also apply to Sleeping characters. These sheets use the path-equivalent reading: **Effort while Sleeping, Hope while Awakened**. This is recorded in the handover as a source erratum requiring canonical cleanup.
