# Final Audit — Hearts in the Void

**Date:** 23 July 2026  
**Rules base:** VeilFarers Corebook 11.35; Keeper’s Companion 11.33; Rust and Steel 11.33

## Outcome

The 17 active Markdown blocks received a final focused mechanics, continuity, agency, and delivery pass. The map-anchored Queen encounter was then integrated and audited against the same rules base. The established adventure structure remains intact, including the terminal-only **“I Need an Idea!” Protocol** and the mandatory no-roll Scene 8 standoff.

## Rules Corrections

- Reframed Maya’s advance as shared mission funding rather than an automatic Wealth Tier increase.
- Clarified that the Credit reward is one shared crew payout and does not automatically change personal Wealth Tier or Crew Wealth.
- Replaced the unsupported “standard Tier 3 light freighter” reference with the current Tier 2 Cargo Hauler profile.
- Removed obsolete `Finesse (Tinker)` and corrected `Finesse (Pilot)` to `Finesse (Pilot/Navigate)`.
- Replaced undefined “moderate Difficulty” with DC 15 (Challenging) where a roll is genuinely needed.
- Restored the complete stabilisation options: adjacent Knowledge (Medicine) or Finesse, DC 15 while Dying and DC 20 while Final.
- Added an explicit Hunger Weaver compatibility erratum: obsolete Fascinated becomes canonical Stunned.
- Excluded the obsolete Charmed-based Weaver Queen stat block.
- Added a local Tier 1 Solo Queen encounter by reskinning the printed Rust-King phased archetype from Keeper’s Companion 11.33, Chapter 12; no unsupported reproductive-caste profile is claimed.
- Removed submitted `save`, named-Difficulty, `Finesse (Tinker)`, hybrid Evasion/HP chase, and unsupported “strong success” language from the Queen material.
- Removed Pearl-Thrift from active mechanics because Corebook 11.35 states that every Veil jump consumes one Quantum Pearl.

## Narrative and Procedure Corrections

- Scene 2 now requires no tension clock to teach the protocol.
- Optional clocks must be visible and have stated fictional triggers.
- Fear dominance or an unrelated failed action does not advance a clock by itself.
- The Queen’s Hunger and Escape Route clocks now advance only from listed causal triggers; filling Hunger reveals a defensive intention and does not force combat automatically.
- The Phase Two Mercy Window remains available, and the optional sliver can be prevented, destroyed, contained, fed, or released.
- Restored **The Ship That Learns** as a full optional recommended ending for continuing campaigns.
- The Keeper decides whether that ending is available; the crew still determines whether the sliver reaches an established physical route to a vessel.
- When achieved, fusion is a genuine campaign fact rather than incidental contamination. It develops gradually, may become familial, and gives Kurogane a continuing reason to pursue the crew.
- Early removal remains possible; after deep integration, separation becomes a dangerous Long-Term Project or campaign objective rather than an impossibility imposed to preserve the hook.
- Fusion grants no automatic control, ownership transfer, free Veil jump, or other unreviewed numerical benefit.
- “I Need an Idea!” remains a terminal backup and never rewinds clocks, losses, or costs.
- Korren’s betrayal remains preventable, delayable, or transformable.
- Silk remains negotiable, Zara’s plan remains changeable, and ARIA-7 remains savable.
- Ship acquisition retains multiple routes and never becomes an automatic reward.
- Scene 8’s social standoff remains explicitly roll-free.
