Known production boundary
-------------------------
The live production Firebase project was not modified or exercised by this validation.
Cloud/session testing used a local or mocked browser harness.

PBKDF2 prevents plaintext password storage and creates a practical entry gate, but
client-side verification is not server-enforced authorization. A publicly readable
Firestore session document can expose its verifier to offline guessing. Production
privacy still requires authenticated membership, Firestore Security Rules, Keeper-role
write restrictions and preferably Firebase App Check.
