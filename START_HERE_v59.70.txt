VEILFARERS PORTAL v59.70 — START HERE
=====================================

PURPOSE
-------
v59.70 stabilizes the release-candidate UI and live table connection.
White Stone is temporarily disabled. VeilFarers Blue is the only active theme.

DEPLOY
------
1. Extract VeilFarers_Portal_v59.70.zip.
2. Deploy the extracted package root to a Netlify Deploy Preview.
3. Do not deploy a parent folder containing the package root.
4. Confirm /data/deploy-version.json reports v59.70.
5. Confirm the HTML response header reports X-VeilFarers-Release: v59.70.
6. Hard refresh or use a private window to prevent old CSS/JS cache contamination.

REQUIRED LIVE ACCEPTANCE
------------------------
- No White Stone selector is visible; every page loads in VeilFarers Blue.
- Human Inheritance of the Builders is chosen entirely in Equipment.
- Experience rows and numeric fields have no overlapping up/down controls.
- Player and Keeper tokens are compact and visually distinguish their states.
- Expanded Effort/feature panels do not repeat the collapsed summary.
- Clicking the 3D overlay during motion does not interrupt the roll.
- Clicking anywhere in the overlay after settlement closes it.
- An expired Identity session stops polling and shows a sign-in/reconnect message.
- After signing in and reconnecting, Keeper ↔ Player updates work in both directions.

AUTHENTICATION NOTE
-------------------
The console errors supplied for v59.69 show an expired or invalid Netlify Identity session:
- /.netlify/identity/token returned 400;
- /api/table-api returned 401.

v59.70 retries once with a forced token refresh. If Identity cannot refresh the session, polling stops and the user must sign in again. This is deliberate; cloud table requests are never sent anonymously.

HUMAN WORKFLOW
--------------
Select Human in Ancestry. Continue normally.
In Equipment:
1. choose The Graft or The Tool;
2. select the exact inherited augmentation or item;
3. choose normal starting weapon and armor.
The inherited choice remains free and does not consume normal starting budgets.
