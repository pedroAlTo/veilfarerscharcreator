Output one polished image only.
Subject: a fictional long, angular science-fiction energy carbine with a bulky coil shroud, ceramic outer panels, a worn skeletal stock and a large abstract power cell. A broad dominant side profile across the middle, with a smaller three-quarter outline and a few offset exterior casing panels below. Schematic styling only: show exterior housings, cables and abstract sci-fi components, not a functional real-world firearm mechanism. The silhouette should be long and grounded, industrial and believable in a far-future salvage setting.
