# Alien Species cultural plates

Built-in ImageGen generation and two targeted ImageGen edits, 13 September 2026. The existing `first-contact.webp` plate supplied the line's engraved navy/brass art direction. The nine new illustrations replace ancestry portraits in “Never a monoculture” and in its field index. Each is available as a complete 1536 × 1024 WebP plus a 768 × 512 responsive variant in `public/assets/books/alien-cultures/`. Generated PNG originals are copied to `output/imagegen/alien-cultures/`.

Public cultural context comes from the 12.06 Alien Species PDF: Humans p. 6; Synthetics p. 7; Rift Spinners p. 8; Kryx p. 9; Kryllians p. 11; Celestiari p. 12; Ethereals p. 13; Bloomstriders p. 14; Ailurans p. 15. The particular rooms and objects are visual interpretations of these practices, not newly asserted canonical artifacts. Keeper secrets and adventure resolutions are excluded from the card text.

The author additionally defined individual Rift Spinner markings and their relationship to clothing and privacy. See [Rift Spinner art direction](rift-spinner-art-direction.md). Crystallis's marked surveillance image is applied only to that subject; the other five subjects continue using the unchanged original surveillance sheet. The Corebook's two Vanguard appearances share `public/assets/books/classes/class-09-vanguard-marked.webp`.
