## Rule references

- Corebook 12.06, chapter 16, PDF pages 151–155: the six Houses and Free Fleet have six reputation levels; the Explorers' Guild describes narrative progression without numbered levels. The public selector preserves that distinction. Older character-creator affiliation data is not used or modified.
- Keeper's Companion 12.06, PDF page 23: the Reactor Meltdown clock advances on its stated roll outcome or printed feature, not simply with time. The UI animation is an explicitly labeled example; the slider permits manual exploration.
- Keeper's Companion 12.06, PDF pages 49 and 59: a Micro environment plus one Macro feature and an optional Veil feature. The exploded illustration presents layers of one scene, not separate floors.
