VEILFARERS PORTAL v59.76 — START HERE

1. DEPLOY
   Deploy the complete contents of VeilFarers_Portal_v59.76.zip from the ZIP root.
   Do not deploy a parent folder containing the portal.

2. VERIFY RELEASE AUTHORITY
   - Open /data/deploy-version.json and confirm v59.76.
   - Inspect an HTML response and confirm X-VeilFarers-Release: v59.76.
   - Confirm X-VeilFarers-CSP-Profile: wasm-3d-dice-v2.

3. CREATOR CARD ACCEPTANCE
   - Select a card in Ancestry, Community, Class and Affiliation.
   - Confirm 48 particles and the animated gold frame are visible on the selected front.
   - Press [+] and confirm the same particles and frame remain visible around the blue reverse.
   - Confirm particles remain clipped inside the rounded card border.
   - Press [-] or click outside and confirm the card returns to neutral with no residual field.
   - Confirm only one card can remain expanded.

4. DICE REGRESSION CHECK
   - Start a 3D roll and press anywhere on the full overlay before settlement.
   - Confirm physical dice disappear and the identical roll resolves in 2D.
   - Let another 3D roll settle, then click the overlay and confirm its result is preserved.

5. SERVICE CHECK
   - Sign in with Netlify Identity.
   - Connect one Keeper Tool and one Player Tool to the same table.
   - Confirm no repeated 401 polling loop appears.
   - Confirm live table updates flow in both directions.
