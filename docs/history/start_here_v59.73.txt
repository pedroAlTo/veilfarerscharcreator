VEILFARERS PORTAL v59.73 — START HERE

1. DEPLOY
   Upload the complete contents of VeilFarers_Portal_v59.73.zip to the normal Netlify deployment workflow.
   Do not deploy selected files over an older folder.

2. VERIFY THE RELEASE
   Open /data/deploy-version.json and confirm v59.73.
   Inspect the HTML response header and confirm X-VeilFarers-Release: v59.73.
   Hard-refresh after deployment.

3. THEME
   VeilFarers Blue is the only active theme.
   White Stone remains deliberately disabled.

4. CREATOR ACCEPTANCE
   - Inspect all card decks; cards inside each deck must remain the same height.
   - Select one card: the card itself lifts, glows and receives a stronger border.
   - Select another or click outside: the previous card returns to neutral.
   - Click [+]: the card lifts further and rotates to a blue, image-free, full-text reverse.
   - Click [-] or outside: it rotates back and returns neutral.
   - Create a Human; choose The Graft or The Tool in Equipment.
   - For The Tool, choose the exact eligible TT3 item and verify The Stubborn Relic text.

5. LEVEL UP ACCEPTANCE
   - Verify equal option-card sizing and the same select/flip behavior.
   - Apply a level.
   - Confirm the Build Changes panel flashes, dissolves into particles and reveals the exact changes applied.
   - Repeat with reduced-motion enabled.

6. CHARSHEET ACCEPTANCE
   - Verify Level, Evasion, Armor and Proficiency do not overlap.
   - Verify Armor shields remain beside Evasion.
   - Compare HP states: purchased/available, marked and not-yet-purchased must be distinct.
   - Verify Hope, Effort and Resolve geometry.

7. DICE ACCEPTANCE
   - Start a 3D Duality roll and click anywhere before it settles.
   - 3D must stop and the same dice must roll automatically in 2D.
   - Repeat with a generic pool and attack+damage append.
   - Click after a result appears; the HUD must close while preserving the result.
   - Start another roll and confirm the saved 3D preference was not changed.

8. TABLE COMMUNICATION
   Sign out and back in before testing.
   Connect Keeper and Player to the same table.
   Confirm updates flow in both directions and no repeated 401 polling appears.
   Expire/sign out one session and confirm polling stops with a single login-required state.

9. DOCUMENTS

   - GITHUB_DEPLOY_SUMMARY_v59.73.md
   - PORTAL_HANDOVER_v59.73.md
   - VALIDATION_v59.73.txt
