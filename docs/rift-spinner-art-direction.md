# Rift Spinner visual continuity

Author's direction, 13 September 2026: every depicted Rift Spinner has individual body markings. They are concentrated on the abdomen and torso, and may extend onto legs and face. Patterns distinguish individuals; some Rift Spinners wear clothing to conceal part of their identity.

Depict these as intrinsic pigmentation within the crystalline surface, visible beneath its highlights. Facet edges, reflections, cracks, armor engraving and floating effects do not substitute for markings. Keep each individual's pattern consistent between appearances, and distinct from other individuals.

Preserve the line's established crystalline arachnid anatomy. Do not transfer the Kryllians' four-arm description to Rift Spinners, turn them into humanoids, or replace their body with a costume. Clothing must fit their actual body and allow their articulated legs to move. A garment can conceal markings while leaving some identifying regions exposed.

Current visual identities: Oracle Crystallis has irregular branching bands and separated dark islands; the Corebook Vanguard has interrupted rings and tapered slashes. Cultural scenes may introduce other individuals with their own patterns. This direction also applies to subsequent illustration prompts.
