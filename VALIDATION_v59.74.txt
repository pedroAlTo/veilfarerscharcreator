3D DICE INPUT ROOT CAUSE
------------------------
v59.73 left `.vf-dice3d-stage` at `pointer-events: none` while visible.
The full-stage handler could therefore not receive the requested arbitrary click/tap.

v59.74 correction:
- `.vf-dice3d-stage.is-visible` has `pointer-events: auto`.
- The visible stage has `touch-action: none`.
- Stage dismissal is handled on capture-phase `pointerdown`.
- `preventDefault`, `stopPropagation` and `stopImmediatePropagation` are applied.
- The close button owns its own capture-phase pointerdown and does not double-trigger the stage.
