THIS ITERATION
--------------
The 3D roller now uses a CSS/JavaScript quantum-acquisition HUD that follows the original physical dice while they move. A camera-aperture scan identifies the higher Duality die; the lower branch powers down to gray. Discarded damage dice receive a staged quantum-vector shutdown and the existing renderer-backed darkening.

Weapon damage dice now roll in the same initial physical throw as Hope and Fear. Critical-only extra damage dice and rules-mandated Brutal rerolls are appended only when actually required.

The visual identification pipeline does not remove, replace or reroll settled dice.
