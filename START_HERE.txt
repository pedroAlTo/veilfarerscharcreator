DEPLOY
------
1. Preserve the current v59.50 branch/deploy and Firestore Rules.
2. Create or update a test branch from the intended base.
3. Replace the branch contents with the CONTENTS of the v59.51 ZIP.
4. Confirm index.html remains at repository root.
5. Do not commit node_modules, .env files or service-account JSON.
6. Commit/push and wait for all Netlify checks.
7. Hard-refresh the Deploy Preview, sign out and sign in.
